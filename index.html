How to read this map

About this dashboard

Explore where the UK's high-growth companies — scaleups — cluster, who funds them, and how they break down by sector and region. Use the tabs at the top; the Map tab is interactive.

On the map

  • Dots are individual companies, coloured by sector.
  • Shaded areas outline a cluster — companies concentrated in one place.

How to use it

  1. Open the Clusters dropdown and pick sectors; hover a cluster to highlight it, click for details.
  2. Use Regions, Investors, Primes and Procurement to filter and overlay.
  3. The other tabs give charts and breakdowns for the same data.
