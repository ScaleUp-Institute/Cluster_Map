How to read this map

About this map

This map shows where the UK's high-growth companies — scaleups — cluster geographically, grouped by sector. Use it to explore which sectors concentrate where across the country.

What you're looking at

  • Dots are individual companies, coloured by sector.
  • Shaded areas outline a cluster — companies concentrated in one place.
  • Grey dots are companies not in a defined cluster.

How to use it

  1. Pick sectors from the chips on the right to show where they cluster.
  2. Click a cluster or company to see details — counts, employment, and more.
  3. Open National Sector Stats for headline figures, or Investors to see who backs each sector.

Clusters


  • Clusters of projected scaling businesses have been identified using HDBSCAN methodology by the ScaleUp Institute, working with data platforms to analyse the locations of existing scaleups, scaling firms and businesses projected to scale.
  • Scaleups follow the OECD definition: businesses with at least 10 employees at the start of the period and average annual growth of more than 20% in employment or turnover over three years.
  • Scaling pipeline businesses follow the Eurostat definition: businesses with at least 10 employees and average annual employment or turnover growth of more than 10% over three years.
